Our Story

Founded in 2020, EcoMarket began with a simple idea: shopping should be a force for good. Our founders, passionate environmentalists and tech entrepreneurs, saw an opportunity to revolutionize e-commerce.

We started as a small marketplace connecting local artisans who used sustainable materials with environmentally conscious buyers. Today, we've grown into a global platform serving thousands of customers worldwide.

Despite our growth, our core values remain unchanged: transparency, sustainability, and fair trade practices in everything we do.

Our Journey

Six Years of Growing Impact

From a small idea to a global movement — every milestone driven by our commitment to people and planet.

2020

Company Founded

EcoMarket launched with 50 sustainable vendors and a bold promise: to never list a product that harms people or the planet. We raised $1.2M in seed funding from impact investors on day one.

2021

10,000 Conscious Customers

Reached our first major customer milestone while planting our 5,000th tree and offsetting 20 tonnes of CO₂. Launched our Eco-Impact Dashboard — giving every shopper a live view of their environmental contribution.

2022

Zero-Plastic Packaging Pledge

Eliminated all single-use plastic from our logistics chain, switching every partner warehouse to 100% compostable, recycled-fibre packaging. Became the first e-commerce platform to achieve this across all 500+ sellers simultaneously.

2023

B-Corp Certification Achieved

Achieved certified B-Corporation status with a score of 128 — well above the 80-point threshold. This recognition confirmed our governance, worker welfare, community impact, and environmental performance meet the gold standard of ethical business.

2026

Global Expansion & 50K Trees

Now serving customers in 50+ countries, with 3,200+ artisans supported, 50,000 trees planted, and 120 tonnes of CO₂ offset. Our community of conscious shoppers continues to grow — and so does our impact on the world.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Four uncompromising principles that guide every decision we make — from the products we list to the partners we choose.

Planet First

Every business decision is filtered through a single question: does this protect or harm the planet? We refuse to compromise on environmental integrity for profit — ever. Our operations, supply chains, and partnerships are all designed to regenerate natural ecosystems, not deplete them.

People Matter

We believe commerce should uplift — not exploit. Every supplier in our network pays living wages, ensures safe working conditions, and shares in the value they create. We audit every partner annually and publish those results publicly, because accountability is not optional.

Radical Transparency

Greenwashing is the enemy of progress. We publish full supply-chain audit reports, carbon calculations, and cost breakdowns for every product. Our customers deserve to know the true story behind what they buy — and we're the only marketplace that shows it all.

Community Power

Sustainable change doesn't happen in isolation. We invest 1% of all revenue into community-led environmental projects chosen by our members. From reforestation to ocean cleanup, our community votes for the causes that matter most — and we fund them.

Environmental Action

Our Environmental Commitments

Concrete, measurable pledges — not vague promises. Here's exactly what we're doing for the planet.

100% Renewable Energy

All EcoMarket operations — servers, offices, and logistics hubs — will run on verified renewable energy by 2027. We've already reached 68% and are on track.

One Million Trees

For every order placed, we plant a tree in partnership with verified reforestation programs across Kenya, Brazil, and Indonesia. We've planted 50,000 so far — 1M is our 2030 target.

Ocean Plastic Removal

We fund the removal of 1kg of ocean plastic for every order shipped. In 2025 alone, our community helped remove 8,500 kg of plastic from ocean-bound waterways in Southeast Asia.

Carbon-Neutral Shipping

Every delivery is carbon-offset at the point of purchase. We partner with Gold Standard"“certified carbon programs to ensure every tonne of shipping emissions is matched by verified climate action.

Plastic-Free by 2025

Zero single-use plastic across all orders. All packaging is compostable, recycled, or reusable. We've already diverted 8.5 tonnes of plastic from landfill — and we're just getting started.

Circular Economy Model

We operate a take-back programme on all eligible products. Customers return used items, we refurbish or responsibly recycle them, and issue eco-credits for the next purchase — closing the loop completely.
