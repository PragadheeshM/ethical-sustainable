Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries about our sustainability services.

How long does the B-Corp certification process take?

Typically, the process takes 6 to 12 months depending on your business size and current impact score. Our Accelerator package can often reduce this by 3-4 months through prioritized documentation.

Can small businesses benefit from Impact Analytics?

Absolutely. Small businesses often have the most to gain by identifying waste early. Our "Starter" analytics are tailored specifically for brands with under 50 SKUs.

Do you offer custom reporting for ESG requirements?

Yes, our Enterprise tier includes fully custom CSR and ESG report authoring that complies with international standards like GRI and SASB.

What makes your "Green Logistics" different?

We don't just buy carbon credits. We optimize shipping routes to reduce actual fuel consumption and partner with EV delivery fleets in major urban hubs.
